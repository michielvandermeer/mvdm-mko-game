# Crafting & Expedition Game

A live-service game in which the player runs a smithy in their own town, crafts gear,
and sends explorers out with that gear to bring back materials — which become better
gear, for more dangerous places. Players trade what they make with each other.

## Language

### People

**Master Smith**:
The player's own character, resident in the Town. Personally crafts the gear that
matters; does not go on Expeditions.
_Avoid_: player character, avatar, blacksmith, hero

**Artisan**:
A hired resident of the Workshop who produces bulk inputs. Never leaves the Town and
is never at risk.
_Avoid_: worker, employee, staff, smith, crafter

**Explorer**:
A named, persistent character sent on Expeditions. Gains skill over time and dies
permanently. A distinct population from Artisans — the two are never interchangeable.
_Avoid_: adventurer, hero, runner, worker, unit

### Places

**Town**:
The player's persistent home base. Holds the Workshop, the Explorer roster, and
everything the player has built.
_Avoid_: base, settlement, hub

**Workshop**:
The building inside the Town where crafting happens and Artisans work.
_Avoid_: smithy, forge, shop

**Area**:
A destination an Expedition is sent to, carrying a difficulty and a tier of Materials.
_Avoid_: map, zone, dungeon, region, level

### Activity

**Expedition**:
One dispatch of one Explorer to one Area. Runs in real time, resolves whether or not
the player is present, and returns a report.
_Avoid_: run, mission, map, sortie, quest

**Crafting**:
The Master Smith's act of turning Materials into Gear. The only source of Gear in the
game.
_Avoid_: forging, production, manufacturing

**Salvage**:
Breaking Gear back down into Materials. The only way to reclaim value from Gear the
player no longer wants.
_Avoid_: scrap, recycle, disenchant, vendor, sell to NPC

### Things

**Gear**:
A crafted, equippable piece carried by an Explorer. Lost permanently when its Explorer
dies.
_Avoid_: item, equipment, kit, loadout piece

**Material**:
A consumable input to Crafting, obtained as Loot or from Artisans. Carries a tier.
_Avoid_: resource, ingredient, ore, component

**Loot**:
What an Expedition brings back from an Area.
_Avoid_: drops, rewards, spoils, haul

**Marketplace**:
The venue where players trade with each other. Player-to-player only.
_Avoid_: auction house, trade board, shop, store
